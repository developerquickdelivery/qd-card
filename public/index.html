PILLAR 01

Resolving "1-on-1 Meeting" vs "Trade Fair"

The Answer: You do NOT have to choose one over the other. The software engine is identical; only the physical surface changes:

  • In 1-on-1 meetings: The user presents via their phone screen widget or taps a sleek physical NFC card. The exchange is intimate and personal.
  • In Trade Fairs & Expos: The user wears the QuickCard QR on their conference lanyard badge or displays the booth table tent. Strangers scan freely from 1.5 meters away.
  • The Winner: Build for Trade Fairs first because trade fairs generate high-volume proof, instant word-of-mouth, and group enterprise sales.
PILLAR 02

Why the "SMS Exchange" Idea Was Flawed

You correctly diagnosed the logical contradiction: "Asking someone for their phone number so you can text them your phone number makes no sense."

  • If you already have their number, the exchange already happened.
  • SMS costs money locally (Ethio Telecom airtime) and creates awkward typing friction in noisy rooms.
  • The Replacement: The "Send Contact Back / ተገናኝ" button on the QuickCard profile. The stranger types their Telegram handle or phone directly on the webpage, or taps "Open in Telegram", eliminating manual SMS friction entirely.
PILLAR 03

Ethiopian Monetization Model (Telebirr & Enterprise)

Individual consumers in Addis Ababa have low willingness to pay subscriptions. Where the real money is:

  • B2B Enterprise Team Accounts: Companies (Banks, Real Estate Developers, Insurance, Importers) pay ETB 1,500 – 3,000 / staff member / year for a branded company portal, unified staff profiles, and lead analytics.
  • Physical NFC / Badge Hardware: Sell custom UV-printed NFC matte cards and acrylic booth stands for ETB 800 – 1,500 one-time fee.
  • Payment Integration: Direct Telebirr merchant integration for automated annual renewals without international Visa/Mastercard barriers.

3 Easy Ways to Install on Your Real Phone (Under 60 Seconds)

Set up physical Quick Settings access on Android, iPhone 15/16, or older iOS models.

🤖 Android Quick Tile (Samsung / Pixel / Xiaomi)

Turn QuickCard into an official Quick Settings tile in your notification pull-down shade:

  1. Open Chrome and navigate to /widget
  2. Tap browser menu (⋮) → "Install app" or "Add to Home Screen"
  3. Swipe down from the top of your screen twice to expand the Quick Settings panel
  4. Tap the Edit (Pencil) icon and drag the QuickCard Tile into your top 6 quick toggles
  5. Now you can tap the tile anywhere—even on your lock screen—to instantly pop up your card!

🍎 iPhone Action Button / Control Center (iOS 17 & 18)

Bind QuickCard to your physical hardware Action Button or iOS 18 Control Center:

  1. Open the built-in Shortcuts app on your iPhone
  2. Create new shortcut: Action → "Open URL" → enter your widget URL qc.et/widget
  3. Go to Settings → Action Button → select your new QuickCard Shortcut
  4. (For iOS 18): Pull down Control Center, tap "+", and add the Shortcut widget directly to your control grid
  5. Press your hardware button anytime for instant haptic card presentation!

👆 Double-Tap Phone Back (Any iPhone / Android)

Present your card invisibly by tapping twice on the back of your phone chassis:

  1. On iPhone: Settings → Accessibility → Touch → Back Tap → Double Tap → Select "QuickCard Shortcut"
  2. On Android: Settings → System → Gestures → Quick Tap → Open app → Select "QuickCard"
  3. Whenever you meet a client at a trade booth, simply tap the back of your phone twice with your index finger
  4. The QuickCard HUD slides up like magic with zero screen taps required!
